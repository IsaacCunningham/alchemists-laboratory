How the System Works

The short version, for players and DMs alike.

The Eight Essences

Every ingredient and every potion is described by eight numbers: Humor (healing/damage), Vigor (physical prowess), Lucidity (mind and perception), Natura (nature and body), Weave (magic itself), Flux (change and transformation), Aether (the planar and unseen), and Calor (heat and force). Together they form a single point in an eight-dimensional space — that's what the eight-spoked sigil on every card is showing you.

Brewing

Combine ingredients and their essences add together. A technique is then applied — some simply scale the result up or down, others bend it toward a particular character. Whatever potion sits closest to the resulting point in that eight-dimensional space is what gets brewed.

Rarity

Ingredient and potion rarity both run Common → Uncommon → Rare → Very Rare → Legendary → Artifice. Higher-rarity potions generally take higher-rarity ingredients, or a large, carefully chosen mix of lesser ones.
